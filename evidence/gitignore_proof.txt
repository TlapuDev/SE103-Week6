What git status showed about the ignored files:

The files remain on my computer but are not included in the changes ready to commit.
I checked the ignore rules with git check-ignore to verify that all three files were ignored.

Why committing secrets is dangerous:
Secret files can contain API keys, passwords, or tokens that give access to accounts and services.
If a secret is pushed to GitHub, other people may copy and misuse it.
Deleting the file later may not remove it from the repository's commit history.
Keeping secrets out of commits helps protect accounts, data, and services.
